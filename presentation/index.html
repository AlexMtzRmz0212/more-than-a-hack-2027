The idea

A ball in possession moves differently.

Speed, acceleration, direction changes and time spent near a body separate three kinds of movement. Only one of them is a hand-over.

Carried

Low speed, small repeated touches, never more than a stride from one player. Clear control.

Passed

Fast, straight, decelerating smoothly from one teammate to another. Nobody is near it mid-flight, and possession has not changed.

Loose

Sharp direction breaks with no settled touch: deflections, rebounds, aerial duels. Possession stays with the last team until someone controls it.

Dead balls are slow and unpressed.

When play is stopped the ball sits still about 2.5 times as often, and the nearest opponent is ~17 m away instead of 5–8 m. That is a dead-ball detector the baseline does not have.

Barça keep the ball further from pressure.

With Barcelona in possession the nearest Athletic player is 7.5 m away; with Athletic in possession Barcelona close to 5.4 m. And the nearest player is the wrong team on 22% of live frames.

The other half of the signal

Possession is a team shape, not a touch.

  • Support triangles. The team with the ball opens passing lanes around it: two or three teammates at 8–15 m, at different angles. Tiki-taka is exactly this.
  • Pressure radius. The team without it closes in. Who is converging on the ball says who does not have it.
  • Shape. The possessing team stretches wide; the defending team compacts. Width and depth change sides when the ball does.
  • Works with no ball. These cues survive the 41% of frames where the tracker lost the ball.

What I would build next

From movement to H, A, D.

01 · FEATURES

Per frame

Ball speed, acceleration, curvature; distance to nearest player of each team; time within reach; support and pressure geometry.

02 · FILL THE GAPS

Physics for a missing ball

Rolling deceleration and simple flight models bridge short gaps; player geometry votes through long ones.

03 · STATE MACHINE

Dead · controlled · loose

A hidden-state model with minimum durations and hysteresis. A loose ball keeps the last team, as the rules require.

04 · LABEL

One label per frame

Control by a new team for long enough is a hand-over; everything else is not. Fewer invented spells, higher spellF1.

Validation on other matches with their own labels; the provided game is only predicted, never trained on.
